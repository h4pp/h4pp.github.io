*Effective date: 11 June 2026*

**Fotio** is a light meter for film cameras. It is designed to work entirely on
your device.

## The short version

Fotio **collects no personal data**. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash
reporting, and no third-party SDKs. It makes **no network connections** —
nothing you do in the app ever leaves your iPhone.

## Camera

Fotio uses your iPhone's camera to provide the live viewfinder and to derive
the scene exposure for metering, and to capture framed stills. The camera feed
is processed **on-device, in real time, and only while you are using the app**.
It is never recorded to a server or shared with anyone. Camera access
(`NSCameraUsageDescription`) is the only permission Fotio requests.

## Your data stays on your device

- **Captured shots** are saved to an in-app gallery and stored locally on your
  device. They are **not** written to your system Photos library and are **not**
  uploaded anywhere.
- **Custom film cameras** and your app preferences are also stored locally on
  your device.

This data lives on your device (and in your device's own backups, if you use
iCloud or encrypted device backups — those are governed by Apple, not by
Fotio). Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

## Third parties

Fotio does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking
services, and does not share data with anyone — because it does not collect or
transmit data in the first place.

## Children

Fotio does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a
new effective date.
